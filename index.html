What I do in a product team

Evidence behind product decisions.

01 / DECIDE WHAT TO BUILD

Shape the roadmap.

Since March 2025 I have reviewed five to seven project proposals in every six-week planning round. Several of my own proposals entered the roadmap, including the MCP integration, trial limits and a restructure of the UI library.

02 / FRAME THE PROBLEM

Question the problem, not only the solution.

In May 2026 I was asked to validate a new prototyping feature. The usability sessions and usage data pointed to a different problem, so the team paused the work and redefined it.

03 / GET IT RIGHT BEFORE RELEASE

Test with developers before launch.

I work alongside developers while they build, test implementations before release, and co-wrote functional specifications with the Head of Product. My pre-release review of a new text editor found critical usability problems, and the team postponed the launch to fix them.

A little about me

From research
into product.

I spent ten years at a small, product-led B2B SaaS company. I started in community and content, then created the research role and was the company’s only researcher for eight years. I worked directly with the founder, the Head of Product and the developers, from deciding what to build to testing it before launch.

I’m now looking for roles in product management and product research, and I’m completing a Product Management master (Product Heroes) and a Product Owner certificate. Based near Bologna, I work in English, Italian and Spanish.
