How I can help your team

From an open question to a clearer next step.

01 / FIND THE PROBLEM

Understand what deserves attention.

I build and maintain an understanding of who customers are, how they work, and what they need. That evidence helps the team frame problems and decide where to focus.

02 / ASSESS THE IDEA

Learn before committing further.

I lead research to evaluate product ideas and test the assumptions behind them. The findings give the team a basis for deciding what to develop, change, or investigate next.

03 / BUILD THE PRACTICE

Make learning easier to sustain.

A sustainable research practice gives the team regular access to customers and the skills to learn from them. I establish recruitment and shared resources, coach colleagues, and connect research to product planning.

A little about me
